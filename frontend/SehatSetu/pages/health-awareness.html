Women's Health

Women's Health Through Every Life Stage

Understand health needs from adolescence to menopause and older age.

  • Adolescents may need guidance about puberty, periods, nutrition and emotional changes.
  • Adults should pay attention to nutrition, physical activity and routine check-ups.
  • Pregnancy requires appropriate antenatal care and advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Menopause can cause changes in periods, sleep, mood and bone health.
  • Preventive care depends on age, symptoms and personal risk.

हिंदी: किशोरावस्था से मेनोपॉज़ तक महिलाओं की स्वास्थ्य संबंधी ज़रूरतें बदलती रहती हैं। नियमित जाँच और सही जानकारी मददगार होती हैं।

Reference: WHO — Women's Health

Breast Health

Breast Health and Warning Signs

Learn about breast changes and when to seek medical advice.

  • Seek assessment for a new lump, unusual nipple discharge, skin dimpling or persistent changes in shape.
  • Many breast changes are not cancer, but new changes should be assessed.
  • Ask a healthcare professional about screening appropriate to your age and risk.

हिंदी: स्तन में नई गाँठ, त्वचा में बदलाव या असामान्य स्राव होने पर जाँच करवाएँ।

Reference: WHO — Breast Cancer

Women's Nutrition

Anemia and Iron Deficiency

Learn about tiredness, iron-rich foods and when testing may be needed.

  • Anemia may cause tiredness, weakness, dizziness or breathlessness.
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding, low iron intake and other conditions may contribute.
  • Iron-rich foods include lentils, beans, leafy greens and fortified foods.
  • A blood test may be needed to confirm anemia. Do not start high-dose supplements without advice.

हिंदी: लगातार थकान, चक्कर या साँस फूलने पर जाँच करवाएँ। केवल लक्षणों से एनीमिया की पुष्टि नहीं होती।

Reference: WHO — Anaemia

🩸 Menstrual Health | मासिक धर्म स्वास्थ्य

Menstrual Cycle

Understanding the Menstrual Cycle

Learn about periods, hormones, ovulation and cycle variation.

  • Day 1 of the cycle is the first day of menstrual bleeding.
  • The cycle involves menstrual bleeding, follicular development, ovulation and the luteal phase.
  • Cycle length varies and can change with age, stress, illness and hormonal conditions.
  • Tracking period dates, bleeding and pain can help identify changes.
  • Repeatedly missed periods or significant changes deserve medical advice.

हिंदी: मासिक चक्र का पहला दिन पीरियड्स शुरू होने का दिन है। बार-बार अनियमितता होने पर जाँच करवाएँ।

Reference: NHS — Periods

Period Care

Period Pain and PMS

Understand cramps, mood changes and ways to manage common symptoms.

  • Symptoms may include cramps, bloating, breast tenderness, headaches and mood changes.
  • Gentle movement, rest and a warm compress may help some people.
  • Severe, worsening or persistent pain needs medical assessment.
  • Sudden severe pain or pain with fainting requires urgent care.

हिंदी: बहुत तेज़ दर्द या रोज़मर्रा के काम में रुकावट होने पर डॉक्टर से सलाह लें।

Reference: NHS — Period Pain

Menstrual Hygiene

Period Hygiene and Product Safety

Learn about sanitary pads, menstrual cups and safe hygiene practices.

  • Choose menstrual products that are suitable, comfortable and affordable.
  • Change products according to product instructions and your flow.
  • Wash your hands before and after changing products.
  • Clean reusable products according to their instructions.
  • Avoid harsh or scented cleansers that irritate the genital area.

हिंदी: पीरियड्स के दौरान साफ़-सफाई रखें, हाथ धोएँ और अपनी सुविधा के अनुसार सुरक्षित उत्पाद चुनें।

Reference: UNICEF — Menstrual Hygiene

When to Seek Care

Heavy or Missed Periods

Understand changes in bleeding and when medical advice is important.

  • Heavy bleeding can have several causes, including hormonal changes and other conditions.
  • Missed periods may be associated with pregnancy, stress, weight changes or hormonal conditions.
  • If pregnancy is possible, consider a pregnancy test.
  • Very heavy bleeding with dizziness, fainting or severe weakness needs urgent assessment.

हिंदी: पीरियड्स मिस होने या बहुत अधिक रक्तस्राव के कई कारण हो सकते हैं। गंभीर लक्षण हों तो तुरंत चिकित्सा सहायता लें।

Reference: NHS — Periods

💗 Reproductive Health | प्रजनन स्वास्थ्य

Reproductive System

Understanding the Female Reproductive System

Learn about the uterus, ovaries, fallopian tubes and their functions.

  • The ovaries release eggs and produce hormones.
  • The fallopian tubes help transport an egg toward the uterus.
  • The uterus is where a pregnancy can develop.
  • Persistent pelvic pain, unusual discharge or unexpected bleeding should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

हिंदी: अंडाशय, फैलोपियन ट्यूब और गर्भाशय प्रजनन तंत्र के महत्वपूर्ण अंग हैं। लगातार दर्द या असामान्य रक्तस्राव की जाँच करवाएँ।

Reference: NHS — Health Conditions

Fertility

Ovulation and Fertility Basics

Understand ovulation and why fertile days vary from person to person.

  • Ovulation is the release of an egg from an ovary.
  • The timing can vary, even for people with usually regular periods.
  • Calendar estimates and apps cannot guarantee the exact day of ovulation.
  • Seek professional advice for fertility concerns.

हिंदी: ओव्यूलेशन का समय हर चक्र में बदल सकता है। केवल कैलेंडर या ऐप के अनुमान पर गर्भधारण रोकने के लिए निर्भर न रहें।

Reference: NHS — Fertility

Family Planning

Contraception and Family Planning

Explore general information about pregnancy prevention and informed choices.

  • Options include condoms, oral contraceptives, intrauterine devices and implants, depending on availability and suitability.
  • Condoms can help reduce the risk of many sexually transmitted infections and pregnancy.
  • Methods have different benefits, risks and effectiveness.
  • A qualified healthcare professional can help identify an appropriate option.

हिंदी: गर्भनिरोध के कई तरीके हैं। सही विकल्प व्यक्ति की ज़रूरतों और स्वास्थ्य पर निर्भर करता है।

Reference: WHO — Family Planning

Sexual Health

Sexual Health and STI Prevention

Learn about consent, sexually transmitted infections and confidential healthcare.

  • Consent must be freely given and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Some sexually transmitted infections may not cause obvious symptoms.
  • Condoms, appropriate testing and timely treatment can reduce risks.
  • Seek advice from a healthcare professional about testing and treatment.

हिंदी: कुछ यौन संक्रमणों में स्पष्ट लक्षण नहीं होते। ज़रूरत के अनुसार जाँच करवाएँ और सुरक्षित संबंधों की जानकारी लें।

Reference: WHO — STIs

🩺 PCOS, PCOD and Other Conditions

PCOS & PCOD

PCOS and PCOD: What to Know

Learn about common symptoms, diagnosis and treatment options for PCOS and PCOD.

  • PCOS may affect periods, skin, hair growth and ovulation.
  • Symptoms may include irregular periods, acne or increased facial hair.
  • Similar symptoms can have other causes, so symptoms alone cannot confirm PCOS.
  • Diagnosis and management should be discussed with a qualified clinician.

हिंदी: अनियमित पीरियड्स, मुहाँसे या चेहरे पर अधिक बाल कई कारणों से हो सकते हैं। PCOS की पुष्टि के लिए चिकित्सकीय जाँच ज़रूरी है।

Reference: WHO — PCOS

Women's Health

Endometriosis

Understand common symptoms of endometriosis and when to seek medical advice.

  • Endometriosis occurs when tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus.
  • Symptoms may include severe period pain, persistent pelvic pain or pain during sex.
  • Symptoms vary and can have other causes.
  • Seek medical advice if pain is severe, persistent or affects daily life.

हिंदी: बहुत तेज़ पीरियड दर्द या लगातार पेल्विक दर्द को हमेशा सामान्य न मानें। डॉक्टर से जाँच करवाएँ।

Reference: WHO — Endometriosis

Cancer Prevention

Cervical Cancer and HPV

Learn about HPV, cervical cancer screening and prevention, including vaccination.

  • Persistent infection with certain high-risk HPV types can cause cervical cancer.
  • HPV vaccination helps prevent infection from important cancer-causing types.
  • Screening recommendations depend on age and health guidance.
  • Unusual bleeding, including bleeding after sex, needs medical assessment.

हिंदी: HPV टीकाकरण और सही समय पर स्क्रीनिंग सर्वाइकल कैंसर की रोकथाम में मदद कर सकते हैं।

Reference: WHO — Cervical Cancer

🤰 Pregnancy and Motherhood | गर्भावस्था और मातृत्व

Pregnancy Care

Healthy Pregnancy and Antenatal Care

Learn about prenatal check-ups, healthy habits and warning signs during pregnancy.

  • Arrange antenatal care early and attend recommended appointments.
  • Discuss supplements, vaccines, medicines and existing conditions with your clinician.
  • Eat a varied diet and follow advice about food safety.
  • Seek urgent care for heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache with vision changes or breathing difficulty.

हिंदी: गर्भावस्था में नियमित जाँच करवाएँ। रक्तस्राव, तेज़ पेट दर्द या गंभीर लक्षण होने पर तुरंत चिकित्सा सहायता लें।

Reference: WHO — Maternal Health

Postpartum Care

Recovery After Childbirth

Learn about physical recovery, emotional well-being and warning signs after childbirth.

  • Recovery takes time and needs rest, nutrition and follow-up care.
  • Seek medical care for heavy bleeding, fever, worsening pain, chest pain or breathing difficulty.
  • Persistent sadness, anxiety or difficulty coping deserve professional support.
  • Thoughts of self-harm require immediate help.

हिंदी: प्रसव के बाद माँ के शारीरिक और मानसिक स्वास्थ्य दोनों की देखभाल ज़रूरी है। गंभीर लक्षण हों तो तुरंत मदद लें।

Reference: WHO — Maternal Health

🥗 Nutrition and Healthy Lifestyle | पोषण

Nutrition

Balanced Diet for Everyday Health

Learn how fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein and water support good health.

  • Include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, pulses and suitable protein sources.
  • Drink safe water regularly.
  • Limit excess salt, added sugar and highly processed foods.
  • Nutrition needs vary with age, pregnancy, activity and medical conditions.

हिंदी: विविध और संतुलित आहार, पर्याप्त पानी और नियमित शारीरिक गतिविधि अच्छे स्वास्थ्य में मदद करते हैं।

Reference: WHO — Healthy Diet

Healthy Lifestyle

Exercise, Sleep and Daily Habits

Discover how regular movement, enough sleep and healthy routines support well-being.

  • Choose physical activities suited to your ability and health.
  • Break up long periods of sitting with movement.
  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Ask your clinician about exercise restrictions during pregnancy or illness.

हिंदी: नियमित गतिविधि, पर्याप्त नींद और लंबे समय तक बैठे रहने से बचना स्वास्थ्य के लिए लाभदायक हो सकता है।

Reference: WHO — Physical Activity

🧠 Mental Health | मानसिक स्वास्थ्य

Mental Health

Stress, Anxiety and Depression

Learn about common signs of emotional distress and when to seek professional support.

  • Stress can affect sleep, mood and concentration.
  • Persistent sadness, loss of interest or excessive worry may need professional assessment.
  • Talking with a trusted person may help but does not replace treatment when needed.
  • If there is immediate risk of self-harm, seek emergency help.

हिंदी: लगातार उदासी, घबराहट या रोज़मर्रा के काम करने में कठिनाई होने पर मानसिक स्वास्थ्य विशेषज्ञ से बात करें।

Reference: WHO — Mental Health

🛡️ Disease Prevention | बीमारी से बचाव

Disease Prevention

Dengue and Mosquito-Borne Illnesses

Learn how to prevent mosquito bites, recognize warning signs and reduce breeding sites.

  • Remove standing water from containers and surroundings.
  • Use suitable repellents, screens and protective clothing.
  • Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding or unusual drowsiness with suspected dengue requires urgent care.
  • Avoid self-medicating; some medicines can increase bleeding risk in dengue.

हिंदी: मच्छरों से बचाव करें और पानी जमा न होने दें। बुखार के साथ गंभीर लक्षण हों तो तुरंत चिकित्सा सहायता लें।

Reference: WHO — Dengue

Preventive Care

Vaccination and Preventive Check-ups

Understand how recommended vaccines and routine check-ups can help protect your health.

  • Vaccines help protect against specific infectious diseases.
  • Recommendations differ by age, pregnancy and medical history.
  • Keep vaccination records where possible.
  • Screening tests depend on age, symptoms and individual risk.

हिंदी: टीकाकरण और सही समय पर स्वास्थ्य जाँच बीमारी से बचाव में मदद कर सकते हैं।

Reference: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, India

🩹 First Aid and Medical Help | प्राथमिक उपचार

First Aid

Recognizing Medical Emergencies

Learn to recognize emergency warning signs and when to seek immediate medical help.

  • Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, sudden facial drooping, new weakness on one side or difficulty speaking can be emergencies.
  • Unconsciousness, heavy bleeding or a severe allergic reaction also needs urgent help.
  • Call local emergency services; in India, 112 is the national emergency number.
  • Do not delay emergency care while searching online.

हिंदी: सीने में तेज़ दर्द, साँस लेने में गंभीर कठिनाई, अचानक कमजोरी या बेहोशी होने पर तुरंत आपातकालीन सहायता लें। भारत में 112 पर कॉल किया जा सकता है।

Reference: India — Emergency Response Support System
